Generated with `sprite-gen gen --provider codex` through GPT image generation on 2026-09-11.
The user authorized the current ChatGPT account. No reference images were supplied.

- `championship.webp`: a team lifting a trophy under stadium floodlights, navy/cream uniforms and golden confetti.
- `overseas.webp`: a player carrying an equipment bag through a tunnel toward a new ballpark at sunset.

Both images are 1672 × 941, with no lettering or team marks. Generated PNG originals were visually reviewed,
then encoded as WebP at quality 84 without resizing. Combined runtime payload: 383,564 bytes.
Original PNG files and sprite-gen reports remain in the ignored `artifacts/moments/` directory.

Championship art is shown only in a winning season report. Overseas art appears with an overseas offer
and immediately after signing a posting / MLB challenge contract. Text remains outside the images for
readability and accessibility. An image loading failure leaves the text card usable.
